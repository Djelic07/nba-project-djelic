# NBA Team Performance Analysis

## Research Question
What statistical differences exist between top-performing and average NBA teams?

## Overview
For this project, I wanted to look at what separates top NBA teams from average teams using team statistics across multiple NBA seasons. Since basketball has so many different stats, I was interested in seeing which numbers actually seem to matter the most when it comes to winning games consistently.

Using Python I collected, cleaned, combined, analyzed, and visualized NBA team data from Basketball Reference.com

## Data Sources
The data for this project came from Basketball Reference team statistics from the following NBA seasons:

- 2020
- 2021
- 2022
- 2023
- 2024

The project used:
- Basic per-game team statistics
- Advanced team statistics
- Team win/loss records

## Tools Used
- Python
- pandas
- matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## What the Project Does
The notebook:
- Imports NBA CSV datasets
- Cleans and merges the datasets together
- Calculates win percentage
- Classifies teams as Top Team, Average Team, or Below Average
- Compares team statistics across categories
- Creates visualizations to show the differences between teams

## Visualizations Included
The notebook includes charts comparing:
- Basic team statistics
- Shooting percentages
- Advanced metrics like offensive rating, defensive rating, and net rating

## Results
The analysis showed that top-performing teams consistently outperformed average teams across most statistics. The biggest differences appeared in advanced metrics such as net rating, offensive efficiency, defensive rating, and margin of victory.

The results suggest that successful NBA teams separate themselves through balanced and efficient play on both offense and defense, rather than relying on only scoring more points. Guess having an allstar caliber player can't do all the work!
